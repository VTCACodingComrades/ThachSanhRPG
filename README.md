# Thach Sanh RPG 2D

## Story Background
ThachSanhRPG is inspired by a beloved Vietnamese folk tale about Thach Sanh, a legendary hero on an epic quest to rescue a princess from a fearsome ogre. Drawing from traditional Vietnamese mythology, the game follows Thach Sanh's perilous journey, challenging players to overcome numerous obstacles and defeat powerful enemies.

## Game Concept
A 2D pixel-art RPG that brings the classic Vietnamese folk story to life, blending traditional narrative with engaging gameplay mechanics. Players step into the shoes of Thach Sanh, navigating through treacherous environments, battling monsters, and ultimately confronting the ogre to save the princess.

- Comprehensive player movement system: idle and walking
- Environment interacting: pushing and interaction capabilities
- Interact with NPCs
- Advanced combat mechanics

### Enemy System
- Diverse enemy types inspired by mythological creatures
- Challenging enemy behaviors
- Health system
- Strategic attack and movement patterns

### World and Environment
